The MIT `LICENSE` applies to this repository's software, workflow and documentation code. It does **not** replace the licences that apply to source data.

## Curlie

Curlie's directory data is licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)** licence. Curlie requires attribution when its content is used.

Required text attribution:

> With content from Curlie.org - the largest human-edited directory of the web. Contribute by submitting a website or becoming an editor.

Sources:
- https://curlie.org/docs/en/license.html
- https://curlie.org/docs/en/rdf.html

## Wikidata

Wikidata structured data in the main, Property, Lexeme and EntitySchema namespaces is made available under **CC0 1.0**. Wikidata does not require attribution for CC0 data, although attribution is appreciated.

Sources:
- https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access
- https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## This project's data contribution

To the extent the project owner has copyright or database rights in the manual factual domain entries and in the project's own selection/arrangement, those rights are dedicated under **CC0 1.0**. This dedication does not remove or weaken Curlie's CC BY 3.0 attribution requirement for Curlie-derived material.

The generated `blocklists/shopping.txt` is therefore distributed with the Curlie attribution above and the Wikidata provenance documented here and in the README. No additional restrictions are imposed by this project on the underlying facts.
